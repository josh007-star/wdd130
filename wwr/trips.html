Our River Trips

Thunder Gorge Beginner Float

Enjoy a relaxing introduction to rafting with gentle Class II rapids and beautiful river scenery. This trip is suitable for beginners and families who want to experience the outdoors with a guide.

Difficulty: Easy, Class II

Duration: Half day

Price: $85 per person

Canyon Run Intermediate Rush

Take on faster water and exciting Class III and IV rapids as you travel through a dramatic river canyon. This adventure is designed for participants looking for a more challenging rafting experience.

Difficulty: Moderate, Class III–IV

Duration: Full day

Price: $150 per person

Devil's Drop Extreme Expedition

Experience a demanding multi-day river adventure featuring powerful Class V rapids. This trip is intended for experienced rafters who are prepared for a challenging expedition.

Difficulty: Extreme, Class V

Duration: Two days

Price: $320 per person
